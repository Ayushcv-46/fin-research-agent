## Bull Case

## Bear Case
- Operating earnings fell sharply from $32.3 B in 2024 to $19.0 B in 2025 due to elevated medical cost trends and the impact of restructuring, portfolio actions, and cyber‑attack related costs. (Section: Item General)
- The company incurred significant restructuring and other action costs—real‑estate rationalization, workforce reductions, and contract‑reserve provisions—raising its operating cost ratio and adding pressure on profitability. (Section: Item General)
- Medical costs rose from IRA‑driven Medicare Part D changes, higher patient acuity, and value‑based care contract adjustments, increasing the medical cost ratio (MCR) and eroding margin. (Section: Item General)

## Summary
UnitedHealth Group’s revenue growth across both UnitedHealthcare and Optum segments is supported by a diversified, integrated business model and expanding member base. Nonetheless, significant earnings compression, elevated operating costs from restructuring and portfolio adjustments, and rising medical cost pressures create challenges for sustaining profitability.
